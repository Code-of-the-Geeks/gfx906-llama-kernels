What did not work (published so you can skip it)

  • Activation-LUT kernels: 20–26 GB/s — dead ends on gfx906.
  • LUT gathers beat by ALU recompute three independent times (sign-table variants measured +21 to +37% slower).
  • VDR=2, 1 or 4 warps per block, loop unrolling: no gain; __umul24 lowers to quarter-rate v_mul_lo_u32.
